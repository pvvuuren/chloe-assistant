Chloe Assistant

Chloe Assistant is a private, personal assistant tool used only by Paul van Vuuren. It is not offered to the public.

With Paul's permission, it connects to his own Google account to help him:

  • sort, read and draft replies to his email (Gmail)
  • view and schedule events in Google Calendar
  • find and organise files in Google Drive, Docs and Sheets
  • look up his own contacts

Nothing is sent, deleted or shared without Paul approving it first.
